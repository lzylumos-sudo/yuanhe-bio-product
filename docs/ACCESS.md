# Access and publication policy

Effective: 2026-10-29.

## Public

This independent repository contains product descriptions, capability boundaries and nonsensitive collaboration information. It starts with a clean history and is not a fork or mirror of the research repository. Do not copy research branches, tags, release packages or internal documentation into it.

## Private research

Research code, virtual-screening implementation, membrane preparation, toxicity and synthesis assessment, internal configuration, tests, deployment tooling, historical code and source releases belong in the access-controlled research repository. Access is granted to authorized collaborators as needed. Public product updates must be reviewed as an explicit list of files; automatic synchronization from the research repository is not permitted.

## Rights and confidentiality

Private visibility limits repository access; it does not recover previously downloaded copies or cancel historical license grants. Third-party software, weights and data retain their own licenses. Commercial use and distribution require an asset-specific rights review. No legal entity, exclusive ownership of upstream methods or completed commercial clearance is asserted by this repository.

Do not submit confidential research, credentials or customer information through public issues.
