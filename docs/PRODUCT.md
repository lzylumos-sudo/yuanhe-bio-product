# Product scope

## Inputs and deliverables

A research engagement starts with the biological question, permitted input structures and molecules, computational budget, review requirements and experimental follow-up plan. Deliverables may include a reviewed candidate list, supporting computational evidence, exclusions, analysis records and a reproducible result index. Exact scope depends on validated capabilities and the project agreement.

## Human review

Researchers retain responsibility for input suitability, interpretation, candidate selection and experimental decisions. Missing evidence is reported as missing, not presented as a successful calculation. A valid outcome can be that no candidates meet the agreed criteria.

## Demonstration boundary

The public material illustrates capabilities and user workflow. It contains no customer data, internal scoring rules, model weights, simulation parameters, unpublished candidates or fabricated performance results. Public examples and performance claims require a separate evidence and disclosure review before publication.

## Delivery planning

Research evaluation and controlled deployment can be discussed with maintainers. Multi-tenant SaaS, guaranteed turnaround, regulatory validation and commercial asset rights are not implied by this product overview.
